## البرومت الأصلي (حرفيًا)

```text
RESHAPE — PREMIUM MOTION GRAPHICS CAMPAIGN

YOUR ROLE

Act as an award-winning Creative Director, Senior Motion Designer, 3D Art Director, Arabic Advertising Scriptwriter, Sound Designer, and Remotion Video Engineer.

Your mission is to PRODUCE AND RENDER a complete, premium, cinematic motion-graphics advertisement for the Saudi sports brand RESHAPE.

This is a professional commercial production, not a slideshow, animated PowerPoint, social media template, or typography-only video.

Brand: RESHAPE

Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/reshape.sa/

Website: https://reshape-sa.com/

Campaign concept: “من أول حركة… إلى أقوى نسخة منك”

Duration: 40–45 seconds

Output: Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 FPS, high-quality MP4.

Language: Saudi Arabic.

Audio: Premium Saudi male voice-over and cinematic sound effects. Absolutely NO background music.

1. BRAND ANALYSIS AND ASSET REVIEW

Before creating anything, inspect all supplied brand assets, logos, videos, and reference materials.

Analyze the official brand identity, services, audience, brand personality, and visual language.

RESHAPE specializes in structured physical training, with a strong focus on calisthenics, bodyweight strength, control, mobility, and skill progression.

The brand also includes RESHAPE Kids, focusing on the physical development, movement skills, coordination, and progressive training of children.

This advertisement promotes real in-person training, NOT an online training platform.

Use the official logo without redrawing or modifying it.

Color System

* Primary dark: #2F3034
* Secondary charcoal: #444448
* Signature orange: #FF5420
* Warm sand: #D9CCC4
* Light neutral: #E9DDD7

Match supplied official assets wherever they differ from these preliminary values.

Visual personality: athletic, confident, disciplined, modern, premium, dynamic, and technically precise.

Avoid childish aesthetics, gaming effects, excessive neon, generic gym advertising, and random animations.

2. CREATIVE DIRECTION

The video must tell a visual story.

Core narrative:

An ordinary movement transforms into a structured journey of strength, control, and achievement.

Introduce one distinctive orange visual element during the opening: a dynamic motion path.

This orange path becomes the visual storytelling device for the entire commercial.

It should transform seamlessly into:

* A calisthenics bar and movement trajectory
* A progressive training pathway
* A functional obstacle course for children
* A visual symbol of progress
* The final RESHAPE logo reveal

Every transition must have a narrative reason.

Create custom illustrated environments and animated objects. Never use plain text as a substitute for a scene that should be visualized.

Use highly polished 2.5D motion graphics, selective 3D elements, kinetic typography, sophisticated lighting, fluid camera motion, and premium athletic graphic design.

All athletes and trainers should be original stylized vector or 3D characters, not stock photographs.

Maintain consistent anatomy, realistic movement mechanics, and clean design.

3. STORYBOARD — 45 SECONDS

SCENE 01 — THE HOOK (00:00–00:05)

A nearly black screen.

A small orange point appears.

A sharp sound effect.

The point moves and draws a dynamic orange trajectory through a dark three-dimensional environment.

As the camera tracks the line, it begins forming the outline of a calisthenics structure.

A stylized athlete begins a basic movement.

Narration:

“مو كُلّ تَمْرِين يِصْنَع قُوّة… ومو كُلّ حَرَكَة تَعْني تَقَدُّم.”

A dramatic but controlled pause.

On-screen typography:

“الحركة ≠ التقدّم”

The text should integrate directly with the moving trajectory, rather than appear as a disconnected title.

SCENE 02 — THE FOUNDATION (00:05–00:12)

The orange trajectory morphs into a real training bar.

A stylized adult athlete performs a technically accurate pull-up.

As the athlete moves, the camera follows the movement arc.

The bar transforms into a series of geometric training platforms.

Each platform represents a stage of progression.

Narration:

“في رِيشَيْب، البِداية الصَّحّ تِفْرِق.”

Show the stages through actual movement demonstrations, not merely labels.

SCENE 03 — BUILDING THE ATHLETE (00:12–00:20)

The camera moves forward along the progression pathway.

Three physical qualities emerge as interconnected scenes:

STRENGTH — athlete executing a controlled bodyweight exercise.

CONTROL — athlete performing a balance or stability skill.

SKILL — athlete progressing toward a more advanced, technically accurate movement.

The orange visual path connects all three stages.

Narration:

“نَبْني الأَساس، نِطَوِّر التَّحَكُّم، وَنِتْقِن المَهارَة.”

Use carefully synchronized typography:

“قوة”
“تحكّم”
“مهارة”

Each word should be supported by a meaningful visual transformation.

SCENE 04 — RESHAPE FOR EVERY STAGE (00:20–00:29)

The motion path splits smoothly into two visually connected environments.

On one side, an adult athlete trains using calisthenics equipment.

On the other side, the visual environment transforms into a premium children’s sports training area.

Create small stylized children performing supervised balance, coordination, and age-appropriate movement exercises.

Show a miniature coach guiding a child through a simple obstacle course.

Avoid framing children as advanced adult calisthenics athletes.

Narration:

“لِلْكِبار اللّي يَبْغُون يَتْقِنُون الكالِسْتِنِكْس… وَلِلصِّغار اللّي تَبْدَأ مَعاهُم رِحْلَة القُوّة وَالثِّقَة.”

The split environments reconnect into a single movement path.

SCENE 05 — WHAT MAKES RESHAPE DIFFERENT (00:29–00:37)

Create a miniature animated training facility.

Use a smooth cinematic camera movement through the facility.

Show an illustrated trainer demonstrating a movement, followed by a trainee practicing it.

Introduce a visual progress pathway with achieved milestones, without invented statistics or results.

Use transformations between training stations, evaluation moments, and progression markers.

Narration:

“تَدْرِيب حُضُورِيّ، خُطَوات وَاضِحَة، وَتَطَوُّر يِنْشاف.”

This sequence must communicate actual guidance and structured training.

SCENE 06 — THE BRAND REVEAL (00:37–00:45)

All previous orange movement paths converge toward the center.

The camera pulls back.

The paths form a dynamic abstract shape before revealing the official RESHAPE logo.

A subtle orange glow travels across the logo.

The background becomes a beautifully lit charcoal studio environment.

Narration:

“رِيشَيْب… مِنْ أَوَّل حَرَكَة، لِأَقْوَى نُسْخَة مِنَّك.”

Then display:

RESHAPE

“ابدأ رحلتك اليوم”

reshape-sa.com

@reshape.sa

Use an elegant, memorable logo finish with a restrained impact sound.

No unnecessary final animation.

Hold the final brand frame long enough to read.

4. VOICE-OVER PRODUCTION

Use a professional Saudi male voice.

Preferred authorized ElevenLabs voice:

Name: mohammed alsagr

Voice ID: 96donzfHvBagGuYj2GLW

Use the configured ElevenLabs integration only when credentials and access are available.

Delivery:

* Authentic Saudi accent
* Confident, premium athletic advertising tone
* Natural, moderately energetic pacing
* Strong articulation
* Controlled emotional progression
* Clear pauses between narrative sections
* No exaggerated shouting
* No robotic delivery

Critical pronunciation:

RESHAPE must be pronounced “رِيشَيْب”, not “ريشاب” or “ريشيب” with unnatural phonetics.

Calisthenics must be pronounced clearly and naturally.

Ensure all Arabic wording and pronunciation are reviewed before rendering the final voice track.

The supplied voice is the preferred narration reference, not an authorization to substitute a random synthetic voice.

5. SOUND DESIGN

STRICT RULE: NO MUSIC.

The production must contain only:

* The authorized male voice-over
* Subtle cinematic whooshes
* Controlled impact sounds
* Soft mechanical movement details
* Exercise-related movement sounds
* Environment ambience when appropriate
* A distinctive final brand sonic impact

Sound design should enhance physical movement and visual transformations.

Never let effects overpower narration.

No musical instruments, rhythmic background beds, artificial trailer music, or hidden soundtrack.

6. ANIMATION QUALITY

Use Remotion as the primary frame-accurate motion composition system.

Create reusable animation components.

Use SVG, custom vector artwork, Rive, Lottie, Three.js, or suitable 3D rendering tools when they materially improve the output.

Use high-quality animation easing, proper anticipation and follow-through, accurate movement arcs, professional motion blur, selective depth of field, coherent perspective, and restrained cinematic lighting.

Every scene must contain original animated visual content.

DO NOT create a video consisting of text over abstract shapes.

DO NOT rely on generic template transitions.

Avoid excessive zooming, glitch effects, random particles, artificial flames, and unnecessary camera shake.

Build the transitions from the actual objects and movements shown in the scenes.

Movement quality matters more than visual clutter.

7. ARABIC TYPOGRAPHY

Use elegant, contemporary Arabic typography compatible with the brand.

Correct right-to-left layout is mandatory.

Use the approved Arabic font if supplied.

Animate short key phrases, not complete paragraphs.

Keep narration, visual action, and on-screen wording synchronized.

Verify correct Arabic spelling, shaping, word order, and punctuation.

Do not display automatic subtitles throughout the entire advertisement unless they improve comprehension without cluttering the composition.

8. PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Execute the following workflow:

1. Review references and official brand files.
2. Create a production-ready storyboard with all scene durations.
3. Design original visual components and illustrated environments.
4. Build the motion-graphics scenes.
5. Generate or integrate authorized narration.
6. Integrate and synchronize professional sound effects.
7. Animate every transition.
8. Produce a complete render.
9. Review the video for design consistency, Arabic accuracy, movement physics, pacing, and audio quality.
10. Correct all visible defects and render the final version.

Do not stop after describing what should be built.

If required production assets or credentials are unavailable, clearly identify the blocker, complete the editable work that is possible, and never claim an unavailable final render has been produced.

9. FINAL DELIVERABLES

Provide:

* Final vertical MP4, 1080×1920, 30 FPS
* Editable Remotion source project
* All generated visual assets
* Approved voice-over file
* Sound effects and synchronized audio mix
* A clean Instagram Reel cover image
* An Arabic Instagram caption with a clear registration CTA

10. FINAL QUALITY STANDARD

The result must look like a premium advertisement produced by a specialist motion-design studio.

It must communicate:

RESHAPE develops strength, movement quality, physical skills, and disciplined progression through structured training.

The viewer should remember the brand, understand its value, and feel motivated to begin training.

The ultimate production requirement: Show the idea visually. Do not merely animate the words.

Prioritize storytelling, memorable visual transformations, brand identity, and professional execution over unnecessary effects.

Begin production using the provided brand references and available tools.
```
